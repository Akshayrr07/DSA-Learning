## Behavior & Steps

1.  **Codebase Scan**: Review `package.json`, tsconfigs, and `wrangler.toml`.
2.  **UI Component Audit**: Check `frontend/src/components/` and `frontend/src/index.css` to locate reusable assets and styling tokens.
3.  **API / Data Flow Mapping**: Trace handlers in `frontend/functions/api/` and queries on D1 database.
4.  **Similar Implementations**: Look up existing DSA problem layouts to preserve structural conventions.
5.  **Documentation**: Log findings to feed the implementation plan.
